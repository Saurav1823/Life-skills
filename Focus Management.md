# Focus Management

---

## Question 1  
**What is Deep Work?**

- Deep Work is working with full focus without distraction  
- It means doing hard tasks with full concentration  
- No phone, no social media, no multitasking  
- It helps produce high quality work   
- It creates more value in less time  

---

## Question 2  
**According to author how to do deep work properly?**

- Schedule time for deep work  
- Remove all distractions  
- Work in a quiet place  
- Set clear goals before starting  
- Focus on one task only  
- Take proper breaks   

---

## Question 3  
**How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?**

- Fix 1–2 hours daily for deep work  
- Keep phone out of reach
- Turn off notifications  
- Make a daily task list  
- Start with most important task  
- Use timer (like 60–90 minutes)  
- Take short breaks between sessions  

---

## Question 4  
**What are the dangers of social media, in brief?**

- Reduces attention span  
- Increases distraction  
- Wastes time   
- Increases anxiety  
- Reduces deep thinking ability  
- Can become addictive
