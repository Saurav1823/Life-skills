## 1) What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

- Unwelcome physical contact.
- Requests or demands for sexual favours.
- Sexual jokes, comments, or remarks about someone’s body or appearance.
- Sending or showing sexually explicit messages, images, or videos.
- Repeated unwanted invitations.
- Staring, gestures, or non-verbal actions of sexual nature.
- Threats, intimidation, or promises of benefits linked to sexual compliance.

---

## 2) What would you do if you face or witness such behaviour?

- Clearly tell the person to stop.
- Document the incident.
- Report to your manager, HR, or the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
- Support the affected person if you are a witness.
- Escalate to higher authorities if the issue is not addressed properly.
